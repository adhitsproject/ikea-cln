# Source evidence and capture limits

Perfect-Web-Clone 3.0.0 was installed from the user-requested repository at commit `c846c37937a70497d37d082c26c85aa1289a9fa5`. Its native extractor failed before browser launch with `PermissionError(13, Access is denied)` while creating a Windows async subprocess pipe. See `capture-error.txt` and `tool-version.json`. The native `capture_integrity` gate therefore failed; the separate reference method below does not substitute for it.

Cloudflare Browser Rendering independently captured rendered HTML and full-page PNGs for all five requested routes at desktop 1440px and mobile 390px. Files are `cloudflare-source/{home,best,stores,faq,about}.html`, matching `*-1440.png`, `*-mobile.html`, and `*-390.png`. `*-layout-1440.json` holds browser-computed positions/styles for the shared header and static page sections. `menu-open-1440.png` captures the source drawer state. Mobile About source content itself overflows to 1080px. These snapshots reflect the live source on 2026-11-11, including its 11.11 campaign.

The implementation follows the captured section order and content: Home hero and campaign media, product/room rails, services and sustainability cards; Best BILLY editorial, category chips, product cards and promo terms; seven stores and fourteen pickup cities; sixty-seven FAQ questions with rich answers; company vision, growth, figures and sustainability. Source media provenance is in `asset-cache/manifest.json`, the seven-video manifest, and Best product download manifest. Local originals are under `public/assets/`.

Cloudflare Browser Rendering also replayed candidate JS/CSS from local production bundles in a remote browser. Candidate screenshot observations, viewport crops, scores, and interaction reports are under `candidate/`. The replay is separate from the blocked native PWC Playwright path. Home hero GIF score captures normalize to the source GIF's frame 1 for timing comparability; the actual app retains the animated GIF. Scores in `run.json` are PWC SSIM on equally sized first-viewport crops with no masks. Full-page score comparisons are diagnostic only when total document heights differ. The app's local cart, search feedback, filters, and menu do not call IKEA account or checkout services.
